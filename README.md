# Misadventures of Samson

**3D endless runner for Android and iOS · Unity 6 (URP) · 2026 · Client project**

A mobile endless runner where Samson dashes through the world while being chased, completing missions and unlocking new outfits along the way. The game was built on Unity's Trash Dash foundation and extended with custom systems for progression and monetization.

## What I built

- **Chase gameplay** layered on top of the core endless-runner loop
- **Mission system** that gives players goals to complete across runs
- **Outfit and cosmetics system** with unlockable character looks
- **Store and in-app purchases** for Android and iOS
- **Mobile optimization** with Unity 6 and the Universal Render Pipeline

## Tech stack

Unity 6 · C# · URP · Unity IAP · Android · iOS
